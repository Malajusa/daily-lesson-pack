# Term 3 Four-Day Overview — Daily Lesson Pack

## Scope

- Applies to Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday.
- Wednesday is excluded unless explicitly requested.
- HASS is excluded from this overview.
- Assume the previous scheduled lesson was taught unless the user explicitly states otherwise.
- Monday follows the preceding Friday unless it begins a new sequence.
- Thursday follows Tuesday.
- Mathematics follows the uploaded Year 4/5 Term 3 mathematics overview.
- Writing explicitly teaches the toolkit students need to write an information report.
- Shared Reading and Guided Reading use separate country texts selected from Europe, Africa, North America and South America.

## Reading rules

- Any country from the four approved continents may be used.
- Shared Reading and Guided Reading must use different countries on the same day.
- Morning Work, Shared Reading and Guided Reading must use independent texts.
- Guided Reading must be pitched to the fixed group for that day.
- Shared Reading should support comprehension and information-text knowledge without replacing the writing lesson.

## Week 1

### Mathematics — Fractions and mixed numerals
**WA code:** WA5MNAUN6

- **Monday:** Review unit fractions and counting beyond one whole; connect improper fractions and mixed numerals using concrete and visual models.
- **Tuesday:** Locate and represent mixed numerals and improper fractions on number lines.
- **Thursday:** Move flexibly between mixed numerals and improper fractions and explain the equivalence.
- **Friday:** Consolidate representation, conversion and explanation through mixed problems and an exit assessment.

### Writing toolkit — Purpose and audience

- **Monday:** Analyse a model information report to identify its topic, purpose and intended audience.
- **Tuesday:** Explicitly teach how factual language and selected details match the report’s purpose and audience.
- **Thursday:** Apply the focus by revising a short report section for a specified audience.
- **Friday:** Edit a completed section to ensure its purpose and audience are clear.

**WA code:** WA5MNAUN6

- **Tuesday:** Compare fractions using benchmark values, especially one half and one whole.

- **Tuesday:** Explicitly teach how to sort research notes and create clear subheadings.
- **Thursday:** Organise students’ own notes into sections and draft the section order.
- **Friday:** Review and improve the order and balance of the report sections.

## Week 5

### Mathematics — Equivalent fractions
**WA code:** WA4MNAUN7

- **Monday:** Use area models and fraction strips to identify fractions that represent the same quantity.
- **Tuesday:** Generate equivalent fractions by multiplying the numerator and denominator by the same whole number.
- **Thursday:** Find missing numerators or denominators and explain equivalence using models and symbols.
- **Friday:** Consolidate and assess recognition, generation and justification of equivalent fractions.

### Writing toolkit — Elaboration with supporting details

- **Monday:** Analyse how a model paragraph develops a main idea with facts, examples and explanations.
- **Tuesday:** Explicitly teach how to expand a simple factual sentence into a developed paragraph.
- **Thursday:** Draft a complete paragraph using a topic sentence and supporting details.
- **Friday:** Edit the paragraph to improve depth, relevance and factual precision.

## Week 9

### Mathematics — Add and subtract fractions with the same denominator
**WA code:** WA5MNAC2

- **Monday:** Model addition of fractions with the same denominator and explain why the denominator remains unchanged.
- **Tuesday:** Add fractions with the same denominator and explain why the denominator does not change.
- **Thursday:** Model subtraction and solve worded or multi-step problems involving like-denominator fractions.
- **Friday:** Consolidate and assess calculation, representation and explanation.

### Writing toolkit — Using evidence and sources

- **Monday:** Analyse the difference between copied notes, paraphrased facts and original explanation.
- **Tuesday:** Explicitly teach how to paraphrase research notes and record the source.
- **Thursday:** Turn students’ notes into original report sentences while retaining source details.
- **Friday:** Check the report for accurate paraphrasing and complete source records.
