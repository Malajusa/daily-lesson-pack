# Year 4/5 contextual profile

Status: calibrated current baseline

## Purpose

This profile preserves the established mixed Year 4/5 instructional pitch while allowing deliberate future refinement. It must not be changed as a side effect of developing Year 6, Year 2/3 or PP/Year 1 support.

The component contracts, visual standards, Mathematics canon and approved regression records remain authoritative for architecture and quality. This file owns only year-level calibration.

## Independence and response expectations

- Students can normally begin Morning Work independently from a projected slide and record responses in books when the runtime classroom context specifies that routine.
- Warm-up questions should be brief, directly actionable and achievable without sustained teacher explanation.
- Student-facing instructions may use accurate curriculum terminology when it is explained plainly and immediately.
- Do not increase difficulty by making instructions adult-sounding, ambiguous or unnecessarily verbose.

## Literacy calibration

- Questions should be immediately understandable to an average mixed Year 4/5 student.
- Multiple-choice distractors should reflect plausible Year 4/5 misconceptions or incomplete understandings rather than obviously absurd alternatives.
- Use accurate terms such as `conjunction`, `pronoun`, `clause`, `preposition`, `prefix`, `suffix` and `topic sentence` when they are relevant, with plain-language support where needed.
- `Subordinating conjunction` is appropriate when that distinction is the learning focus; otherwise `conjunction` is normally sufficient.
- Warm-up tasks normally require one direct response only. Sustained written justification belongs in a main lesson unless explicitly requested.

## Numeracy warm-up calibration

For the mixed Year 4/5 profile:

- Questions 1-4 should remain accessible to Year 4 while still worthwhile for Year 5.
- Questions 5-10 should mainly retrieve Year 4/5 and earlier knowledge.
- `All`, `Most` and `Some` remain three complete questions of increasing complexity.
- `Some` may extend reasoning or abstraction but must not automatically leap into Year 6 content.
- The warm-up retrieves taught knowledge rather than previewing a new procedure merely to create challenge.

## Mathematics lesson calibration

- Use the current user/school overview to resolve the lesson focus and progression; do not treat this profile as a term planner.
- Independent work may require multi-step reasoning when the teaching sequence has modelled the relevant conceptual demand.
- Technical vocabulary and conventional notation should be used rather than replaced with vague language.
- Differentiation should change scaffold, representation, reasoning, abstraction, strategy selection or context rather than merely changing how completely students answer one task.

## Shared reading calibration

- Use one short projected paragraph and one question per question slide.
- Keep text large enough for whole-class projection; split longer texts across additional slide pairs rather than shrinking them.
- Across a sequence, use a mix of literal retrieval, main idea, vocabulary, inference, evidence, cohesion and text-feature questions as appropriate.
- Inference questions must remain anchored in explicit clues rather than requiring unsupported background knowledge.

## Writing calibration

- Use the user's current writing overview/toolkit progression as the source of what is being taught.
- Explicitly model new or fragile features before independent application.
- Students should produce a clear written product demonstrating the targeted feature rather than merely collecting facts.
- Do not assume a fixed Monday/Tuesday/Thursday/Friday Information Report sequence unless the supplied runtime overview defines it.

## Regression protection

Any change to this profile or to shared/core rules that can affect Year 4/5 pitch must apply:

- `examples/benchmarks/t3w6-monday-modular-regression.md`;
- `examples/benchmarks/t3w6-tuesday-release-regression.md`;
- `examples/benchmarks/t3w6-thursday-literacy-regression.md`;
- `examples/benchmarks/universal-maths-canon-regression.md` where Mathematics is affected;
- `examples/benchmarks/year-profile-isolation-regression.md`.

The goal is not to freeze this profile permanently. Deliberate Year 4/5 improvements are allowed; accidental drift from work on another year level is not.
