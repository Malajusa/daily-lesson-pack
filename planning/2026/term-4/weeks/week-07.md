# Week 7 — 23–27 November 2026

## Weekly purpose

- All Term 4 assessment evidence must be complete by the end of this
  week.
- Prioritise genuine catch-up, missing evidence, moderation-ready work
  samples and targeted intervention; do not introduce substantial new
  assessable content.

## English — Writing

**Assessment completion, feedback and next steps**

**Lesson 1 — Absence/catch-up assessment**
- Students absent during Week 6 complete the narrative under equivalent conditions.
- Students already complete undertake independent writing.

**Lesson 2 — Whole-class feedback**
- Use de-identified examples to examine common strengths and needs such as complications, resolutions, paragraph control, sentence repetition and vocabulary precision.
- Do not disclose individual scores publicly.

**Lesson 3 — Improving one craft feature**
- Practise a class-wide need.
- This is post-assessment learning; submitted assessments are not modified.

**Lessons 4–5 — Independent/creative writing**
- Use flash fiction, alternate perspective, unusual setting, retelling or short suspense scenes.

## Mathematics

**Complete assessment evidence**

**Lesson 1 — Address identified misconceptions**
- Group students according to actual Week 6 needs: symmetry, angles, grids/location, nets or 3D properties.

**Lesson 2 — Missing evidence / assessment catch-up**
- Students requiring legitimate completion do so; others complete geometry extension.

**Lesson 3 — Mathematical communication**
- Explain solutions using diagrams, terminology and reasoning.

**Lessons 4–5 — Rich application**
- Use design/spatial challenges combining geometry concepts after formal evidence is closed.

## HASS

**Economics and Business assessment**

**Lesson 1 — Final preparation**
- Revisit resources → scarcity → choice → trade-off → consequence without rehearsing assessment answers.

**Lesson 2 — HASS assessment**
- Use unfamiliar scenarios requiring identification of limited resources, alternatives, choice/trade-off and consequences.
- Prioritise application over definition recall.

**Lesson 3 — Catch-up / discussion**
- Absence completion where needed; other students discuss how priorities/values can lead to different resource decisions.

## Health

**Hygiene assessment**

**Lesson 1 — Applied hygiene task**
- Use scenarios across food preparation, illness, sport/swimming, oral hygiene, shared spaces and changing body-care needs.
- Require explanations of why actions are appropriate.

**Lesson 2 — Catch-up and misconception correction**
- Address misconceptions only after assessment evidence is secured.

## Digital Technologies

**Complete, evaluate and explain**

**Lesson 1 — Final development**

**Lesson 2 — Evaluation**
