## Approved characteristics

- One immediately usable slide with no cover or setup slide.
- A strong title hierarchy, full-height left rail and broad Maths/Literacy areas.
- Four accessible operations, including two-digit-by-two-digit multiplication.
- Current-focus Mathematics retrieval that connects vocabulary, notation and
  conceptual application without teaching a new method.
- Familiar grammar and punctuation revision requiring students to write their
  responses in books rather than mark projected content.
- A meaningful `When finished` extension that deepens reasoning without being
  necessary to complete the 8-10 minute core.
- Clear grouping, comfortable spacing, restrained colour and readable
  Trebuchet MS typography across the 16:9 canvas.
- Instructions state exactly what students must write and can be started
  independently from the projected slide.

## Adaptation rules

Retain the composition and cognitive balance where they fit the current
context. Change all task content to match the active year profile, taught
knowledge and current sequence. Do not force fractions-to-decimals content,
the jetty sentence, the Wednesday date or the exact calculations into another
pack.

If a current context does not support separate Maths and Literacy areas, retain
the exemplar's hierarchy, spacing and executable-in-books standard rather than
copying its two-column structure mechanically.

## Regression use

Use this exemplar after changes to Morning Work generation, typography,
projected-book response rules, panel containment or visual assembly. A future
Morning Work slide passes when it retains or improves the approved
characteristics, contains accurate current content, and passes full-size visual
review. Similarity of colour or layout alone is insufficient.
